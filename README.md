# Privacy Policy for Finora

**Last Updated: September 12, 2026**

Finora ("Finora", "we", "us", or "our") is a personal financial management application designed to help users organize their money, monitor spending, manage savings, track monthly commitments, and make more informed financial decisions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Finora may collect, how that information is used, how it may be stored or processed, and the choices available to users.

By using Finora, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The information Finora collects depends on the features you use.

### Information You Provide

Finora may collect information that you voluntarily enter into the application, including:

* Income and budget information
* Expense amounts and expense categories
* Expense dates and notes
* Savings goals and savings records
* Monthly financial tasks and commitments
* Recurring expenses
* Purchase information entered for purchase analysis
* Financial preferences and settings
* Information required to create or authenticate an account, if account-based features are enabled

Financial information entered into Finora is used to provide the application's financial management features.

### Account Information

If Finora uses account authentication, we may receive information associated with your authentication method, such as your email address, display name, or an account identifier.

We use this information to authenticate you, associate your financial data with your account, and provide access to your saved information.

### Device and Technical Information

Depending on the services and software libraries used by the version of Finora you install, certain technical information may be processed, such as:

* Device type
* Operating system and version
* Application version
* Basic technical information required to operate and secure the application
* Crash or diagnostic information, where applicable

We do not intentionally collect precise location information unless a future version of Finora specifically requires it and provides the appropriate disclosure.

## 2. Financial Information

Finora is designed to process financial information that you choose to enter.

This may include information such as:

* Income
* Budgets
* Expenses
* Spending categories
* Savings
* Financial goals
* Upcoming financial commitments
* Purchase information

This information is used to provide features such as budgeting, spending analysis, savings tracking, financial calculations, purchase analysis, and personalized financial insights.

Finora does not connect to your bank account or automatically access your bank balance unless a future version explicitly introduces such a feature and obtains the necessary permissions.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

* Provide Finora's core functionality
* Calculate budgets, balances, spending rates, and other financial information
* Display spending and savings insights
* Track monthly commitments
* Provide purchase-decision assistance
* Provide savings recommendations
* Maintain your account and saved information
* Synchronize your information across supported devices, where cloud synchronization is enabled
* Maintain and improve application reliability
* Detect and resolve technical problems
* Protect the application against misuse or unauthorized access

We do not use your financial information to provide personalized financial advice from a licensed financial adviser.

Finora provides informational tools and automated insights. It does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

## 4. Artificial Intelligence Features

Some Finora features may use artificial intelligence to analyze information you provide and generate explanations, recommendations, or financial insights.

For example, AI features may help with:

* Understanding spending patterns
* Identifying possible areas for saving
* Evaluating a potential purchase
* Explaining financial trends
* Providing general financial suggestions

Finora's core financial calculations are performed by the application using the information stored in your account or device.

AI-generated responses are informational and may contain errors. Users should review important financial decisions independently.

We do not intentionally instruct the AI system to invent financial information that is not present in the data provided to it.

If AI services are used to process information, relevant information may be transmitted to the AI service provider for the purpose of generating the requested response. The exact data transmitted depends on the AI features enabled in the version of Finora you use.

## 5. Firebase and Cloud Services

If cloud-based features are enabled, Finora may use Firebase or other cloud infrastructure to provide services such as:

* User authentication
* Secure data storage
* Synchronization between devices
* Application infrastructure
* Security and reliability

Information stored using these services may be processed by the relevant service provider as necessary to provide the requested service.

Cloud services may process information using encrypted connections and other security measures. Firebase documentation states that Firebase data collected by its Android SDKs is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, subject to the specific service and configuration used.

The exact Firebase services used by Finora may change as the application develops.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal or financial information.

We may share or transmit information only when necessary to provide Finora's functionality, operate supporting services, comply with legal obligations, protect the application and its users, or process information through service providers that help operate the application.

Examples may include:

* Authentication providers
* Cloud storage and database providers
* AI service providers used by Finora's AI features
* Technical service providers
* Analytics or crash-reporting services, if included in the application

Third-party service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

We do not authorize third parties to use your financial information for purposes unrelated to providing the services described in this Privacy Policy.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Finora.

These measures may include:

* Encrypted network communication
* Authentication controls
* Access controls
* Secure cloud infrastructure where applicable
* Appropriate database security rules
* Limiting access to information to what is necessary for the application to operate

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and authentication credentials.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as it is necessary to provide Finora's functionality or as required for legitimate operational, legal, or security purposes.

Information stored in your Finora account may remain available until you delete it or request deletion, subject to any applicable legal or operational requirements.

If Finora stores information through third-party services, deletion may require processing by those services as well.

## 9. Account and Data Deletion

If Finora provides account-based functionality, users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

To request deletion, contact us using the contact information provided below.

When a valid deletion request is received, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information associated with the account, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

If Finora provides an in-app account deletion feature, users may use that feature instead of contacting us.

## 10. Children's Privacy

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Finora without appropriate permission, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.
